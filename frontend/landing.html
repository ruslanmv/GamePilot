// How it works

Just keyboard and mouse. No memory injection. No file modification.

GamePilot looks at your screen, thinks, and presses keys — exactly like a person. It runs on your machine. It can't read game memory. It can't talk to servers it isn't supposed to.

// 01

Watch

A vision model reads your screen at 24 fps. It sees what you see — HP bars, enemy positions, prompts.

// 02

Decide

An action model picks the next move — dodge, attack, build, mine — and rates its confidence.

// 03

Act

It presses keys. Ctrl + Shift + Esc hands control back to you instantly. Always. No matter what.

// Safety first

Your game. Your time. Your choice.

GamePilot is built to keep gaming fun, not to ship cheats. The whole product is wrapped in guardrails most people will never need to think about — because they're the default.

Emergency Stop
Ctrl + Shift + Esc. One hotkey. Always armed.
Time caps
Default 10 min, hard cap 60. You set it. We enforce it.
Single-player only
Multiplayer and ranked are blocked by design.
Transparent logs
Every keypress AI made — visible, exportable, auditable.
