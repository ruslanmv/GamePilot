# GamePilot Safety & Responsible Use

GamePilot is an **AI gameplay research and experimentation platform**.

## Allowed Focus
- Offline/single-player experiments
- Sandbox and simulation environments
- Accessibility and educational demos
- Vision/action-recognition research

## Not Intended For
- Cheating in online multiplayer games
- Evading anti-cheat systems
- Exploiting online game economies
- Bypassing protections or policy controls

## No Guarantee Statement
GamePilot cannot guarantee protection from account actions by game publishers.
Users are responsible for complying with game Terms of Service and local laws.

## Safety Design Rules
1. Default runtime is **Safe Mode ON**.
2. Session duration is capped in safe mode.
3. High-risk autonomous actions are blocked in safe mode.
4. Emergency stop is always available.
5. Transparent logs are retained for review.

## Public Messaging Guidance
Use:
- "AI gameplay research platform"
- "controlled environments"
- "assistive copilot"

Avoid:
- "undetectable bot"
- "no-ban guarantee"
- "auto-grind for online MMO"
